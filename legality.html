PreFIRE Banlist Philosophy

Deck diversity and game quality are major goals of PreFIRE, and the banlist works to preserve both. As the format grows and decks become more refined, cards may join or leave the banlist to ensure PreFIRE remains healthy. Be sure to join our discord server to participate in ongoing discussions about the PreFIRE ban and watchlist!
